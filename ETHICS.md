# Ethics

## Consent

### Characters in the Film
This film tells a fictional story inspired by Ethiopian cultural values. **No real person's face, likeness, or voice is depicted.**

- The characters are fictional (Addisu's Father, Young Addisu, Adult Addisu, the Elder)
- All images are AI-generated and do not depict any real individual
- The narration voice is generated by Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS (synthetic prebuilt voice "Schedar"), not a real person's voice
- **No voice cloning is used**

### Story Source
The story is an original moral tale inspired by common Ethiopian cultural values about respect for elders and truth-telling. It does not depict any specific real event or real family.

## Labelling Plan

- **End card:** The film includes a visible end card stating "This film was made with AI"
- **Platform disclosure:** When posting on YouTube/TikTok/Meta, the platform's AI content disclosure will be switched on
- **Watermarks:** SynthID and C2PA metadata from generation tools will be left intact
- **Repository:** This ETHICS.md clearly documents all AI tools used

## Privacy

- No private family details are shared in API calls
- The story is fictional and does not reveal personal information
- API keys are stored in .env (gitignored) and never committed

## Style Choices

- Visual style is described by qualities (warm, cinematic, earth tones) rather than referencing any living artist
- No real artist's style is named or imitated
